## Publication rules

- Scientific facts and methods can support independently written activities. Do not copy or closely paraphrase supplier worksheets, distinctive scenarios, answer keys or artwork. See the [Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html).
- Attribution alone does not grant permission, and free educational distribution does not automatically establish fair use. See [fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html).
- Keep Bio-Rad, Carolina and miniPCR manuals on their official websites. The [pGLO manual](https://commerce.bio-rad.com/webroot/web/pdf/lse/literature/1660033_EDU.pdf) permits classroom duplication, which does not establish permission for public website redistribution.
- Use generic lab titles; identify optional kits without their logos or implied sponsorship. Proprietary materials and physical kit procedures remain subject to the supplier's terms.
- The current [Biology 2e preface](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/preface) states CC BY-NC-SA and additional reuse notices. This release does not import its text or illustrations. Referencing the underlying biology does not license third-party expression.
- Preserve Rick's source PDFs unchanged. New revisions to generated packets should continue to distinguish student pages from teacher keys.
