# Biology lab delivery modes and classroom options

Assessed 2026-11-15; generated from [biology-lab-modes.json](data/development-tracker/biology-lab-modes.json). Covers all 48 catalog labs plus the optional Sordaria extension.

**Current implementation:** 14 labs have Virtual + Classroom metadata and existing classroom guidance; 35 currently run virtually without a classroom-entry route. All 33 newly built labs currently run as virtual-only. These are source implementation labels, not claims of a completed classroom pilot.

**Classroom options are planning judgments.** A virtual-only app can have a useful physical, paper-model or supplied-data counterpart. The proposed variants below are not implemented classroom modes or validated protocols. Existing classroom guidance also needs the local teacher setup/pilot described in its guide. A printable worksheet alone does not establish a classroom version.

## The 33 new labs

21 are candidates for an experiment, specimen study, field observation or a physical apparatus with measured results; 12 are better matched to a classroom model, card activity or supplied-data investigation. Both kinds can involve meaningful hands-on work. A physical apparatus or token model must be identified as a model; it does not become a direct measurement of the actual biological system.

## What each classroom adaptation must add

- A question and learning evidence that survive the change of materials or procedure. The bird activity, for example, needs a modeled inheritance/new-generation step; collecting seeds with tools alone is not evidence of inherited population change.
- An original, teacher-reviewed procedure and setup that match the actual equipment, time, quantities and learners. Reviewed external examples support feasibility; they are not automatically adopted protocols or authorization to reuse third-party resources.
- Student collector sheets and teacher guidance that match the physical/model activity, plus optional name/period and a completed PDF/print report. Current virtual-only handouts do not automatically cover a physical variant.
- Clear provenance for measured, provided and simulated data. Real measurements and unexpected results must be retained. Virtual target values are not grading targets for physical observations.
- An explicit decision about classroom data entry or a separate paper/report route, meaningful controls/samples, model limits and a documented teacher pilot before a readiness label changes.
