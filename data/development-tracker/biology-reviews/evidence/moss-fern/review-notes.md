English, virtual only. This review does not establish physical culture/protocol, classroom comprehension, actual hardware/assistive-technology, translated-language or production-release acceptance.

The three learning goals connect process/set tracking, water-dependent fertilization and the contrasting familiar generations in moss/fern. A single fern spore and one compatible sperm event teach distinct transitions; the moss comparison adds different generation/dependency evidence without replaying the fern cycle. No survival, fertilization-rate or independent-sample evidence is invented. Actual student interpretations, generation tags and original writing supply learning evidence beyond navigation.

OpenStax 25.1, 25.3 and 25.4 and UBC's Fern Gametophyte page were read. Auckland fern/moss pages appeared in search but direct fetches failed, so they are not claimed as fully read sources. The model uses a typical homosporous fern and a separate supplied moss example. Meiosis in the 2n spore-producing cell forms n spores. Mitosis grows an n gametophyte and makes n gametes; n sperm/egg fusion gives a 2n zygote. Mitosis grows a young sporophyte, initially attached and later independent in the fern. The leafy moss is an n gametophyte; its attached seta/capsule is a dependent 2n sporophyte, not necessarily wholly nonphotosynthetic. Moss meiosis occurs in the capsule; its early protonema is explicitly omitted. Dominance is not simply tallest structure or evolutionary superiority.

The lens opens an explanatory selected-cell view rather than claiming to reveal chromosomes with a physical hand lens. The four haploid products belong to one selected cell model, not the entire sporangium. Plant shape, organs, gametes and n/2n symbols are at separate scales and are supplied context. Sperm is from a compatible supplied gametophyte. Moisture for growth is distinguished from a later continuous surface water route into the egg-holding organ. Water enables the modeled route, without proving natural success. Guided sperm placement represents swimming, not a physical tool protocol. Detailed chromosome/embryo events and growth times are shortened.
