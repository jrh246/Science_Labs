# Body size and heat exchange — individual build brief

Question: How does size change surface area per volume and passive cooling, and is faster temperature change the same as more total heat released? Three goals: interpret cube area/volume scaling; compare initial/final temperature changes and average rates under matched conditions; distinguish temperature from total transferred energy and apply limits to living animals.
