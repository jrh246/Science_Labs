# BIO-30-BUILD: Investigating Microhabitats

Question: How do conditions and woodlouse counts vary between shaded litter and an open edge?

Collect comparable organism counts and distinguish supplied conditions from observations. Evidence: What did you count, what did the model supply, and what did the quadrat and matched setup keep the same? Why were different locations useful?

Compare means, variation and overlap across locations rather than rely on one frame. Evidence: Use both habitat means, the count ranges and the overlapping value to describe the pattern. Did every shaded location exceed every open location? Compare with your prediction.

Explain an association without claiming that a single factor caused it. Evidence: Relate the counts to temperature and the moisture index. Why can this survey not show that moisture alone caused the pattern? Propose a fair further investigation and name one detection or sampling limit.

Interactions and workload: Set a quadrat and soil probe at the first marked location. Matched setup at the five other locations is then supplied. Each snapshot has the same1m²area, same modeled time and complete visible detection. Counts are of the same modeled woodlouse type. No animals move between snapshots. Three separate, nonoverlapping locations in each habitat show spatial variation. These six locations are not repeated counts of one frame or six controlled experiments. All locations and values are invented teaching data; locations were preselected, not a representative random survey.

Model limits: These are supplied frozen scenes, not field measurements. Organisms are enlarged and separated for counting. Frame area is1m²by model definition; screen size is not a ruler. Soil temperature is in°C. Moisture is an invented relative index from0to100, not percent water, humidity or a calibrated soil reading. Probe depth and observation time are matched by assumption. Litter, light, temperature, moisture and detection can change together in nature. This comparison shows association, not which factor caused it or a universal woodlouse preference. Three locations show only this small supplied set, not a reliable population estimate, seasonal pattern or independent habitat replication. Some real woodlice may be hidden or moving; complete detection and no movement are model assumptions.

Design decisions: Six different1m²frozen locations reveal spatial variation and overlap; demonstrate quadrat/probe once, later matched setups supplied. Original woodlice/counts/conditions are teaching data, moisture relative index not percent; association and preselection limits explicit.
