## Learning and scientific design

Students place a prepared unknown slide, adjust focus, classify actual illustrated shape, add the same unknown to matched plus/minus-glucose media, read acid and gas separately, and combine traits with four invented reference profiles. Each shape and each gas result occurs twice: neither alone uniquely identifies the sample. Yellow occurs in every glucose-positive profile. The no-glucose comparison, positive acid/gas reference and no-cell blank serve distinct purposes. The latter references are supplied, not student observations. Optional matched second preparation reduces routine work without copying interpretations. Several cells in one field are not independent cultures.

OpenStax 22.2 and ASM's Carbohydrate Fermentation Protocol were read for shape/domain limits and acid/gas interpretation. All profiles are invented bacterial fermenters within the defined teaching set; no unknown domain is inferred from shape. The light illustration does not resolve DNA or establish nuclear structure. The inverted gas collector starts liquid-filled; gas displaces liquid near its closed top. Missing visible gas is an endpoint detection result, not proof of zero production. This lab contains no oxygen/growth-pattern assay or live culture instructions.
