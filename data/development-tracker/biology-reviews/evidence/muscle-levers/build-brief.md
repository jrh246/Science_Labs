# BIO-24 — muscles and levers

Question and evidence: compare the same20N load at30cm with cable attachments at2cm and4cm. Student mounts the load, connects the cable at its actual hook, adjusts and tests a pull, reads the force and calculates torque, then explains muscle/tendon biology and model limits. The matched second load is supplied, not a duplicate independent measurement. Two distances add controlled variation; adjustment pulls are search attempts, not biological samples. Goals: torque balance, inverse force/distance comparison, muscle/tendon/antagonist and ideal-model limits. Main misconceptions: balanced forces must be equal, muscle pushes in reverse, moving a virtual hook changes actual anatomy, model force measures student strength.

Source verification: OpenStax CollegePhysics2e9.2 torque/net equilibrium and9.6 muscle/joint sections, A&P2e11.1 tendon pull/insertions.
