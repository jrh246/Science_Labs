# Animal body plans — individual build brief

Question: How can symmetry tests, supplied development records and internal sections distinguish selected animal body plans?

Goals: test mirror planes rather than equating a round outline with radial symmetry; distinguish germ-layer evidence from an external view; distinguish a gut lumen from no body cavity, pseudocoel and true coelom using lining evidence. Five selected examples contribute distinct evidence: irregular sponge, simple eight-tentacle polyp, planarian, straightened nematode and annelid.

Students rotate a mirror plane and test 0/90 degrees for each specimen, place a section lens on the actual body region, probe the gut and surrounding region of the three triploblasts, then classify using all the evidence. No repeated specimen preparation or triple trials. Two perpendicular tests are evidence in these supplied idealized examples, not a proof of all planes or all animal diversity. No physical dissection.

Sources read: OpenStax Biology2e27.2,28.1,28.2,28.3 and University of Michigan ADW Nematoda. Check coelom lining around gut versus wall; do not classify cnidarian digestive space or sponge water spaces as a coelom. Two/three germ layers are supplied developmental records, not inferred from external shape. No evolutionary ladder or phylogeny from one trait; exceptions and other life stages excluded.
