# Flower structure and pollination — individual build brief

Three goals: locate anther/stigma/style/ovary/ovule and distinguish transfer from fertilization; trace a growing pollen tube and model two different fusions; connect zygote/endosperm/maternal coverings/ovary wall to an early seed/fruit with chromosome-set limits. First prediction/controls required before art/actions. Generalized diploid flowering-plant model, no species identification or literal flower-part counts from a photograph.

One no-pollen recipient reference and one matched recipient receiving viable compatible pollen. Model-provided handling excludes accidental/self pollen; donor anthers separate, recipient pollen-bearing anthers excluded. One focused ovule, no default triple trials or success-rate estimate. Brush picks pollen at donor anther, deposits on stigma. Petal deposit is retained as meaningful error, uses up load and does not count as pollination. Required stop-check: transfer has happened, fusion has not. Compatibility and later growth assumed; no claim pollination always guarantees seed/fruit or self-incompatibility universal.

Growth-step marker is an annotation/reveal tool, not a real plant component or a hand steering growth. Place at current grain/tube tip; first snapshot shows tip partway down style, second shows tube extending along modeled maternal route through the micropyle into a receiving synergid. Ring geometry must follow tip. Tube transports two nonmotile sperm; pollen grain does not travel whole into the ovule, no swimming sperm or tube built as string of cells. Provided mature gametophytes; meiosis/mitosis formation omitted.

Inside one enlarged ovule, show egg, central cell with two1n polar nuclei, two synergids and three antipodals. Two delivered sperm cards are interchangeable; student assigns each once to egg/central cell and enters resulting chromosome sets2/3. Invalid duplicate/wrong/empty proposals stay unconfirmed; only checked valid proposal shows fusion products. Egg+sperm→2n zygote; central polar nuclei+sperm→initial3n endosperm lineage. n is sets per nucleus, not cell count or DNA amount. Early developing seed/fruit anatomy then requires four origin interpretations: embryo from zygote2n, endosperm from central fusion initially3n, seed coat from maternal ovule coverings2n, fruit wall from maternal ovary wall2n. Endosperm persistence/seed tissues vary; no exact mature-tissue DNA claim, accessory fruits/parthenocarpy/apomixis excluded.

Institutional sources read: OpenStax32.1 anatomy/development and32.2 pollination/double fertilization; UMN14.1 male/female gametophyte and fusion text; UF IFAS Fertilization; OSU reproductive plant-parts sections. Dresselhaus/Sprunck/Wessel2016 review abstract/introduction and Figure1 caption read for nonmotile cargo/synergid guidance; not full review/figure inspection. Do not import erroneous source wording about ovule wall becoming fruit, eight embryo-sac cells, mature endosperm always persisting or pollination guaranteeing fertilization. All code/art/directions original; no source diagrams copied.
