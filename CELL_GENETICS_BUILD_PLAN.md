## Copyright and licensing boundaries

Scientific facts, ideas, procedures, and methods are not protected by copyright. Particular written explanations, photographs, illustrations, worksheets, and software can be protected. Independently author our instructions, questions, equipment artwork, simulations, and downloads from scientific principles; do not closely paraphrase a supplier lesson or reproduce its distinctive instructional presentation. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html)

Free educational access does not automatically make copying fair use. Attribution is not a substitute for permission. Our default is original material or clearly licensed material, rather than reliance on fair use for downloadable supplier resources. [Copyright Office fair use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html)

Bio-Rad's pGLO manual permits duplication for classroom use only. Treat this as insufficient permission to republish it on our public website; link teachers to the official manual. Purchasing a kit does not establish public redistribution rights for its curriculum. Apply the same item-specific review to Carolina and other suppliers. [pGLO manual](https://commerce.bio-rad.com/webroot/web/pdf/lse/literature/1660033_EDU.pdf), [Carolina terms](https://knowledge.carolina.com/terms-and-conditions/)

The current Biology 2e web preface specifies CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, requiring attribution, noncommercial use, and share-alike for adaptations. It also states additional restrictions concerning AI use. Verify the exact downloaded edition and each reused asset's credit line before any adaptation; do not assume the current webpage establishes the license of Rick's older PDF. Prefer chapter references and links with independently written science explanations. Resolve permission questions before any commercial reuse or AI ingestion of licensed textbook content. [Biology 2e preface and reuse notices](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/preface)

Trademarks, patents, and contractual kit restrictions are separate from copyright. Use generic lab titles and equipment designs, with supplier names only where needed to identify optional classroom purchases. Do not imply sponsorship. No patent clearance has been performed; seek qualified advice if a planned implementation depends on a proprietary process or material, particularly before commercialization. [USPTO distinctions](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/trademark-patent-copyright)

Supplier examples establish feasibility, not permission to copy their materials. Curriculum alignment does not require reproducing textbook expression. Simulated data must be explicitly identified and must not be described as measured experimental results.

1. Complete the existing-asset provenance review and establish an attribution register. Resolve any material to be reused before publication.
2. Build the accessible foundational investigations: chapters 4, 6, 12, and 14. These establish microscope, gas-collection, counting, and liquid-handling components.

## Requirements for every lab

- Start with an original introduction, preparation explanation, and prediction without disclosing the outcome.
- Guide students through preparation, equipment setup, measurement, prominent data collection, and repeated trials or fields. Unlock open exploration after the required comparison.
- Provide realistic, accessible equipment controls; keyboard and touch operation; pause and accelerated time where relevant. Preserve saved progress and distinguish virtual from classroom observations in reports.
- Put classroom options in a collapsed section near materials. Provide method-specific original student worksheets and teacher guides with supplies, timing, preparation, controls, data tables, analysis, troubleshooting, and model limitations.
- Keep models grounded in cited scientific evidence; document assumptions and separate illustrative parameter choices from experimental measurements.
- Review each lab's text and assets for originality and permission before public deployment. Verify downloads, accessibility, mobile layout, data recording, and model behavior, then pilot the physical method with a teacher.

## Completion evidence

Each lab is ready to publish when its rights register is complete for shipped assets, its physical procedure is feasible and reviewed, its virtual behavior is scientifically checked, and its original downloads match the implemented workflow. Record source licenses separately from the eventual license chosen for our own curriculum and code; a repository-wide license cannot grant rights to third-party material.
