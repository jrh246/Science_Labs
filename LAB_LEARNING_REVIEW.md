# Learning and repetition review

Reviewed 2026-11-08. This review covers all 48 catalog entries: 15 available labs and 33 planned entries. It also covers the runnable Sordaria extension and the matter-cycle card activity. Planned entries have descriptions, but no student procedure to shorten yet.

The rule is to require another action or sample when it adds evidence, a new comparison, a different biological outcome, or useful practice. Three trials are not a default. A shorter conceptual model does not establish a reliable population effect: independent replication belongs in a lesson that asks about reliability, uncertainty, or variation.

## What the shortcuts do

A shortcut becomes available only after an earlier virtual sample has been collected. It prepares the next fresh sample with the matched procedure. It does not copy readings, advance simulated observation time, classify specimens, fill tallies, or supply gel estimates. Leaf and yeast trials retain their distinct generated sample parameters. Classroom data cannot unlock virtual procedure completion. Students can still choose the manual setup path.

The reduced enzyme, plant, expression, and gel pathways use separate `streamlined-v2` browser-storage keys. Earlier multi-trial work remains under its original key and can be downloaded from the new workspace. Existing sampling-lab data stay in their original workspaces. DNA has its own separate `extraction-v2` migration boundary.

## Development acceptance rule

Before requiring another trial, name what students will learn from comparing it with the earlier one and include that comparison in the analysis. Use one practice sequence for equipment operation, preserve meaningful decisions and observations, and make further practice available. Distinguish independent samples, repeated measurements of one sample, and several subsamples from one specimen. A model can teach a mechanism with one run per condition; it must not present that as an estimate of biological variability.

The revised worksheets and teacher guides follow these decisions. Method-specific physical fermentation packets and teacher-supplied cutout originals remain unchanged because their repeats support the retained sampling and stoichiometry lessons.
