# Proposed classroom labs and activities

Researched 2026-11-15. **49 labs; 135 options; two or three per lab.**

These are proposed pairings for your review. The first option is a suggested starting point, not an approval. Close matches retain the main question; adaptations change materials or evidence; related-concept options may require a new virtual question/model. Original adaptations are labeled and still need development. Publisher pages and excerpts were checked for relevance; full local protocol, grade-level, accessibility and reuse review remains part of implementation.

Open the [searchable local review page](http://localhost:4310/CLASSROOM_ACTIVITY_OPTIONS.html), or sort/filter the [CSV](data/development-tracker/biology-classroom-options.csv). Current modes and your reviewed-lab marks remain unchanged. Source record: [biology-classroom-options.json](data/development-tracker/biology-classroom-options.json).

**38 related-concept options** include a redesign note. They are alternatives you can select, not a decision to rebuild all of those labs. A published image or digital module is not presented as a ready-made physical lab; its proposed paper adaptation is labeled separately.

## The Chemistry of Life

<a id="seed-germination"></a>

### Seed Germination

Current app: **Virtual** · New build. Prefer a measured germination comparison; a tropism option would change the main question.

**1. Seeds under different water conditions** — Close match · experiment · Published activity/resource

Students: Compare germination and root growth under controlled water conditions. Learning evidence: A fair test, germination counts and an explanation supported by real observations.

**Virtual pairing:** Retain the question; replace fixed virtual timing and doses with an actual growing protocol.

Review source: [SAPS: Do seeds need water to germinate?](https://www.saps.org.uk/teaching-resources/resources/6191/do-seeds-need-water-to-germinate/).

**2. Upright Petri-dish growth investigation** — Adaptation needed · experiment · Published activity/resource

Students: Track roots and shoots through upright dishes and graph measurements over several days. Learning evidence: Separate germination percentage from subsequent growth.

**Virtual pairing:** Add root/shoot measurements and a classroom observation schedule.

Review source: [SAPS: Investigating Seed Germination](https://www.saps.org.uk/teaching-resources/resources/186/student-sheet-5-investigating-seed-germination/).

**3. Rotate seedlings to investigate gravitropism** — Related concept / redesign review · experiment · Published activity/resource

Students: Use the upright-dish method to investigate root growth after the dish is rotated. Learning evidence: Explain a directional growth response rather than whether a seed germinates.

**Virtual pairing:** Redesign around plant responses and growth direction if this becomes the main pairing.

Review source: [SAPS: Investigating Seed Germination](https://www.saps.org.uk/teaching-resources/resources/186/student-sheet-5-investigating-seed-germination/).

## Matches deliberately limited or excluded

- **Modeling the Nephron: Simple sieve or muddy-water kidney filter.** Useful for a filtration analogy, but it does not account for selective reabsorption or secretion. Not recommended as a complete counterpart to the current nephron learning goals.
- **Early Embryonic Development: Human/frog diagrams used interchangeably with the current sea-urchin example.** Organism-specific movements and structures differ. A new context requires a coordinated redesign, not swapped illustrations.
- **Modeling Viral Spread: A digital-only epidemic simulator described as a physical classroom activity.** A website resource is not itself an offline class model. Only an explicitly authored paper/network adaptation would satisfy that goal.
- **Investigate an Unknown Prokaryote: Uncontrolled culturing of unknown classroom/environmental microbes.** Not a proposed option. Prefer prepared evidence, images or a separately approved educational protocol.
